Clara Morel Urban Planner

"Living in Fribourg means noticing the small things — the rhythm of the streets, the quiet corners, the way the city moves with you."

Every day here unfolds gently, almost naturally. I walk through familiar paths that still manage to surprise me, watching how the city shifts from morning calm to daytime motion.

It's in these simple transitions that Fribourg reveals its character — not loudly, but through the details you learn to recognize when you live them day after day.
