Past meets present

In Fribourg, the past and the present coexist every day. This meeting of architectural heritage and contemporary lifestyles paints the picture of a city in motion, experienced and perceived differently by everyone who walks its streets.

Walk down to the lower town in the evening, and the medieval streets take on another rhythm entirely — quieter, slower, lit by a handful of windows and streetlamps. Walk back up toward the station the next morning, and you're back in a city that feels thoroughly modern. Few places manage to hold both timelines at once, so casually.
