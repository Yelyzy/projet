Morning rhythm

"What I appreciate most is how close everything is. In just a few minutes, you can move from a lively street to a quieter space that feels almost untouched by time."

Every morning, the streets of the city center fill with students, workers, and local residents making their way through the city on foot or by bicycle. The bridges connecting its different neighborhoods are a constant reminder of how deeply Fribourg's development has been shaped by its landscape and history — carved into a valley, split by a river, stitched back together by stone and steel.

By 8am, the market stalls near the cathedral are already busy. Bakers know their regulars by name. A group of students cuts through the old town on their way to class, the same route their parents probably walked twenty years earlier. Nothing here rushes — even the morning traffic seems to move at the city's own pace.
