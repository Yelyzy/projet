A city in motion

Fribourg's evolution is not defined by dramatic change, but by a series of small transformations that influence how people move, meet, and experience the city.

The city is also undergoing significant transformation. New residential developments, the growth of sustainable mobility, and the expansion of public spaces are gradually changing the daily habits of its residents. While these changes sometimes raise questions about the future of certain neighborhoods, they also help make the city more accessible and better connected.

Some residents worry about what gets lost along the way — a familiar shopfront replaced, a quiet street suddenly full of scaffolding.

Others see it differently: proof that a small city can keep reinventing itself without erasing what came before. Either way, the conversation about Fribourg's future is one that seems to involve almost everyone who lives there.
