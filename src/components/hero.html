The city through the eyes of those who live it every day.

By Clara Morel · Portrait · 4 min read

Between the medieval facades of the Lower Town and the new neighborhoods emerging on the outskirts, Fribourg continues to evolve without losing its identity. For many residents, this human-scaled city offers a rare balance of heritage, student life, and urban vitality. Every morning, the streets of the city center fill with students, workers, and local residents making their way through the city on foot or by bicycle. The bridges connecting its different neighborhoods are a constant reminder of how deeply Fribourg's development has been shaped by its landscape and history. "What I appreciate most is how close everything is," says Léa, who has lived in Fribourg for five years. "In just a few minutes, you can move from a lively street to a quieter space that feels almost untouched by time."
